# Kotlin acoustic comparison — 2026-10-31

Actual production Kotlin functions were executed by `AcousticReferenceTest`.
Parselmouth 0.4.7 provided genuine desktop Praat references for the identical
quantized PCM16 WAVs. No reference results or fixture audio are embedded in the
application. Machine-readable results: [2026-10-31-praat.json](2026-10-31-praat.json).
Reproduction and methods: [ANALYSIS.md](../docs/ANALYSIS.md).

## Corpus and criteria

Twelve deterministic generated WAVs: four-resonance vowels at 120/220 Hz; a
five-resonance vowel at 120 Hz; 80/440 Hz sine waves; silence; seeded white noise
as an unvoiced negative control; noisy, amplitude-modulated and voiced-transition
variants; a repeated three-minute vowel; and a 1 ms clip. All are mono PCM16 at 48 kHz. No human speech corpus
was available in the repository. Synthetic vowels/noise do not establish accuracy
on male/female speech, fricatives, irregular voices or real microphone noise.

Engineering criteria were defined before comparison: clean pitch median relative
error <2%, paired octave errors <1%, voicing agreement >95% away from boundaries;
stable formant median error <100 Hz or 10% (whichever larger); dynamic intensity
correlation >0.95; pulse median nearest-reference distance <15% of period with no
silence/noise markers. Missing formants do not count as matching values. The
report is not an overall pass or a claim of Praat parity.

| Check | Measured result |
|---|---|
| Pitch, voiced test signals | Median absolute differences 0.012–0.844 Hz, median relative differences 0.015–0.384%; zero paired octave errors. |
| Voicing | 100% agreement at interior frame times for steady fixtures, silence and white noise; 98.82% for voiced transition. Silence/noise: 0/97 frames voiced, no pulse markers. |
| Five-resonance vowel, ceiling 5,500 Hz | F1–F4 median absolute differences **1.08 / 0.20 / 0.49 / 0.70 Hz**, 112/112 paired interior frames per formant. Kotlin median estimates 542 / 1509 / 2478 / 3491 Hz; synthesis resonances 500 / 1500 / 2500 / 3500 Hz. Agreement with Praat does not remove source-model bias. |
| Same vowel, ceiling 5,000 Hz | F1–F3 differences 0.93 / 0.76 / 0.22 Hz; F4 **unavailable for all 111 interior Kotlin frames**, rejected as too broad. Ceiling changes can remove otherwise usable measurements. |
| Four-resonance vowels, ceiling 5,500 Hz | F1/F2/F4 paired median differences ≤1.62 Hz. F3 **unavailable in all 112 interior frames**: order-10 LPC introduces a broad extra pole between physical resonances. Praat retains it; mobile rejects bandwidth >700 Hz, retaining the missing rank. Displayed F4 is the fourth LPC candidate, not necessarily the fourth physical resonance. |
| Noisy vowel, ceiling 5,500 Hz | F1–F4 paired median differences 2.65 / 4.35 / 14.52 / 98.58 Hz; F4 available in 81/112 frames. At 5,000 Hz, F4 available only in 2/111 frames (paired error 58.82 Hz); **poor coverage**, not validated reliable F4. |
| Modulated intensity | Correlation **0.9999992**, median contour residual after constant offset **0.0215 dB**. Praat minus mobile median ≈93.992 dB; these are differently referenced units/window functions. No SPL calibration is inferred. |
| Vowel pulse timing | Median nearest-Praat differences ≈0.0065–0.0134 ms; noisy vowel ≈0.0121 ms. No marker falls outside reference voicing. Mobile produces 115 vs 119 points on 120 Hz vowel, 211 vs 215 on 220 Hz: conservative edge coverage. |
| Pure sine pulse timing | **Fails the phase-distance criterion**: ~6.240 ms at 80 Hz and ~1.132 ms at 440 Hz, approximately half a cycle from Praat. Positive/negative extrema are symmetric; initial polarity choice differs. Cycle spacing remains accurate without accumulated rounding. A sine wave has no identifiable glottal closure. |
| 1 ms signal | No pitch, formant, intensity or pulse frames; no out-of-bounds access or fabricated values. Too short for configured windows. |
| Three-minute vowel | Actual Kotlin WAV loading, FFT spectrogram, pitch, intensity, both formant ceilings, pulses and CSV export completed in **9.048 s** in the desktop JVM. Spectrogram stayed within 20,000 columns. This is not a phone/GPU performance measurement. |

Frame grids are not identical. Kotlin pitch centres begin at 20 ms with 10 ms
spacing at the defaults; formants at ~25.091 ms with ~6.273 ms spacing for 11 kHz
resampling (6.25 ms at 10 kHz); intensity begins at 20 ms with 10 ms spacing.
Praat comparisons query its tracks at the actual Kotlin timestamps and exclude
edges where needed. Pulse nearest-neighbor comparison does not establish
one-to-one closure detection or independently validate the voiced decisions.

## Source verification

```text

```

The warnings concern existing dependency/target/Gradle update suggestions, backup
rules and launcher resources. The JVM suite includes waveform/WAV alignment,
viewport coordinates, tone pitch, silence/noise, known resonances, unavailable
candidate ranks, dBFS power/averaging, pulse timing and cancellation.

No phone/emulator rendering, gesture, persisted-preference, rotation, playback
endpoint or long-file device testing was performed. These remain incomplete
verification, alongside natural-speech scientific validation. Numerical tests
cannot prove a working touch interface on a device.
